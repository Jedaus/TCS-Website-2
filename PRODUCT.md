## Users

Dual track, both primary:
1. **Trade** — builders, carpenters, cabinet installers. Need speed, precision, zero site recutting, reliable local supply.
2. **Homeowners and renovators** — kitchens, wardrobes, vanities. Need a millimetre-exact fit without joinery markups, and easy 3D planning.

## Product Purpose

Total Cabinet Supplies (TCS) manufactures and supplies custom flat pack cabinetry and trade supplies from 68 Compton Rd, Woodridge QLD. **TCS supplies and manufactures; it does not install.** Installation enquiries are referred to a network of vetted Brisbane carpenters and installers.

## Positioning

- vs big-box (IKEA, Kaboodle): millimetre-exact sizing instead of 300/600/900 mm modules and filler strips; Australian Made boards (HMR optional) with Blum hardware; same-day or next-day local remakes instead of 3–4 week waits.
- vs Flatpax Cut To Size: warm, uncluttered, clear pathways instead of a dark, dense factory catalogue.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- goCabinets white-labelled 3D planner and 24/7 trade ordering portal.
- Typical lead time 5–10 business days from specification confirmation; urgent jobs expedited.
- Factory pickup: panels up to 2,400 mm, so a ute, van or trailer with 2.4 m flat cargo length.
- Site delivery across Greater Brisbane, Logan, Gold Coast and SE Queensland; fee by distance and pack volume.
- Woodridge Selection Centre: display counters, Polytec and Laminex finish chips, Blum hardware. Walk-ins welcome.
