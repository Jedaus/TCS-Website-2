## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page owns one idea — the kit is the argument. A flat pack order is a claim about precision, so the page proves completeness the way a trade catalogue proves it: parts, dimensions and quantities as the primary content. It refuses the arrangement this category always ships, a dark hero photograph of a finished luxury kitchen with a craftsmanship line and a quote form, which sells an installed kitchen TCS does not install.

OWN-WORLD: Two inks on uncoated stock. HMR green #0F5C33 and press black #151A17 on #EDEFE9, green owning whole bands and table headers rather than accents. Archivo at condensed width for headings and for one monumental element per viewport; Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for all running and specification text, tabular lining figures in every numeric column. Square corners, hairline rules as the primary divider, index tabs, filled green bands. No cards, no gradients, no photography used as mood.

STORY: A builder understands within seconds that TCS supplies flat pack cabinetry as a pre-cut, pre-drilled, labelled kit from Woodridge, that the parts list is exact, and that TCS supplies rather than installs. They believe nothing will be missing on site. They ring the counter, email a cut list, or come in.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A green masthead band across the full width carrying the name and the counter facts in stock-coloured condensed caps. Beneath it a stock-coloured tab strip on a hairline rule. Then a catalogue entry in two columns: at left, FLAT PACK set monumental in condensed Archivo, one plain sentence under it, and a ruled specification table naming what a kit contains with its numbers in tabular figures; at right, a real TCS job photograph bleeding to the right edge under a thin green rule with a spec-type caption. The primary action sits inline at the foot of the left column as a filled green control carrying the counter number, with emailing a cut list beside it.

FORM: The two-ink Australian trade supply catalogue. Candidate 6 of the grounded list; seed key d571ece0, assigned.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
